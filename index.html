How it works

  1. Pick a game above.
  2. Type what your child is learning this week.
  3. Tap Make my game. Play it on this device, or send the link to another one.

Every game can read words out loud, so children who can't read yet can still play.

Want a different kind of game?

We've written instructions you can copy into an AI chat app, like ChatGPT or Claude. It makes a game about anything your child loves.

Is it safe?

  • No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. We don't ask who you are, and this site has no analytics.
  • Your words stay in the link. The game is saved inside the link itself, not on our computers. We never see what you type.
  • Anyone with the link can play it. So don't type your child's full name, school or address into a game.
  • Free and open. Anyone can see how it's built, and anyone can help make it better.

Kitchen Table Games is free and open source. See the code or suggest an idea on GitHub.
