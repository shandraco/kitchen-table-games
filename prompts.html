What to do next

    
    
      
  1. Open an AI chat app, like Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini. The free versions work.
    2. 
      
  2. Paste the request into the chat box and send it.
    3. 
      
  3. Wait for the game to appear. If you only see code, type: Show me the game so I can play it here.
    4. 
      
  4. Play it yourself first. Check that every answer is right before your child plays.
    5. 
      
  5. Want changes? Just ask in plain words, like "make the buttons bigger" or "add five more questions".

Before your child plays

      
AI apps can make mistakes, including wrong answers. Always play the game yourself first. Don't type your child's full name, school or photos into the chat.
